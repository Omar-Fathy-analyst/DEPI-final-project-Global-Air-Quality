Problem Statement

The Challenge

Raw air quality feeds are long-format, per-species logs with inconsistent casing, mixed units, duplicates, and physically implausible values.

Three Failure Modes

  • Double-counting from unresolved duplicate rows
  • Distorted averages from unvalidated outliers
  • Incomparable scales across pollutant species

Real-World Impact

Without disciplined transformation, downstream analysis risks misleading conclusions affecting:

  • Public health policy decisions
  • Environmental research validity
  • Urban planning and regulation
  • Individual health awareness

Example: Min reading exceeding max, or negative pollutant concentrations — both physically impossible.

Speaker Notes

Set the stage explaining why raw data cannot be trusted. The three failure modes provide a framework. WAQI data is valuable but needs significant preprocessing.

Silver Layer — Cleaning & Transformation

The most substantial pipeline stage. Structured cleaning, validation, and feature engineering.

Standardization

  • Column names → snake_case
  • Country codes → uppercase
  • City names → title case
  • Species names → lowercase
  • Unify wind-gust variants

Validation Rules

  • Drop rows where min > max
  • Remove negative readings
  • Discard count < 1
  • Enforce Q4 2023 date range

Feature Engineering

  • Pivot long → wide format
  • Forward/backward fill gaps
  • Calculate composite AQI
  • Classify: Safe/Polluted/Hazardous

Time Intelligence

  • is_weekday flag (0/1)
  • Month name extraction
  • Season classification
  • Day/week of year

AQI Formula: Mean of four normalized sub-indices — PM2.5/15, PM10/45, NO2/25, SO2/40, O3/100. Thresholds: ≤1.0 Safe, 1.0–2.0 Polluted, ≥2.0 Hazardous.

Speaker Notes

Silver layer is where the magic happens. Highlight wind-gust unification as a real data quality issue. AQI formula is a composite index, not one pollutant.

Challenges Faced & Solutions

⚠

Long-Format Source Data

Each city/date had multiple rows (one per species). Reshaping via pivot while preserving valid readings required careful missing combination handling.

Solution: Group-wise forward/backward filling within each city before pivoting.
🔤

Inconsistent Species Naming

Wind gust appeared as wind-gust, wind_gust, windgust. Would silently split into sparse columns, undercounting coverage.

Solution: Dedicated normalization unifies variants before pivoting.
❓

Missing vs Invalid Data

Missing precipitation = zero (no rain). Missing temperature = estimate from neighbors. Different strategies needed.

Solution: Field-specific imputation, not blanket rules.

Speaker Notes

Real-world data engineering experience. Long-format issue common in sensor data. Naming inconsistency classic quality problem. Imputation challenge shows domain knowledge.
